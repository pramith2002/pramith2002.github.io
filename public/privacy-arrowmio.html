Privacy Policy

Arrowmio: Tap Puzzle Game · Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Arrowmio: Tap Puzzle Game mobile application (the "App") provided by Horizon Builds ("we," "our," or "us"). The App is free to play, supported by advertising and optional in-app purchases.

1. Summary

  • No account is needed. You can play without signing up or logging in, and we don't run our own servers for the App.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Levels, coins, boosters, settings, your multiplayer nickname and profile photo are saved only on your phone.
  • The App uses Google services. Google AdMob shows ads, and Google Firebase helps us understand how the game is played and fix crashes. These services receive some information from your device, described below.
  • Wi-Fi races are local. Your nickname and optional profile photo are shown only to the other players in the same room on your Wi-Fi network. They are never sent to us.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Your game progress, coins, boosters, purchases, settings, your multiplayer nickname and, if you add one, your profile photo are stored only on your device. The profile photo is a small copy (128 by 128 pixels) of a picture you choose with your phone's own photo picker; the App doesn't read any other pictures. All of this is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC, to show banner ads, ads between levels and optional reward videos. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and language
  • Ad interaction data, such as ads viewed or tapped, and diagnostic information

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud and abuse. Depending on your settings and region, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and how Google uses information for advertising.

4. Usage Statistics and Crash Reports (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to improve it, the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records events such as the screens you view, levels you start, win or lose, boosters you use, ads you watch, purchases, Wi-Fi races, your answer to the rating question and when you leave the App. Firebase also records an app instance ID, your device model and system version, and a coarse location derived from a masked IP address. This information is not linked to your name or to an account.

To find and fix crashes, the App uses Firebase Crashlytics. If the App crashes, a report is sent with technical details: where in the code the crash happened, the screen and level you were on, recent game events, your device model and system version, and a crash-reporting installation ID.

5. Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are made through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and the App never receives your card or payment details. The App keeps a record of what you bought so it can give it to you and restore it on a new install. Google Analytics for Firebase also records purchases (the product and its price) for our statistics.

6. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized and before Firebase collects usage statistics. Your choices are stored on your device and passed to Google AdMob and Firebase. You can change them at any time in the App's Settings under Ad choices.

7. Wi-Fi Races

When you host or join a Wi-Fi race, the phones in the room exchange your nickname, your profile photo (if you added one) and your progress in the race, directly over your local Wi-Fi network. Nothing is sent to us, and nothing is kept after you leave the room.

8. Permissions

The App uses only these permissions: Internet and network state (to load ads and send statistics), Wi-Fi state and Wi-Fi multicast (to find and join Wi-Fi races), vibration (for feedback in the game), Google Play Billing (for purchases), and the Advertising ID (used by Google AdMob). Some Google services also add the Google Play install referrer and permissions to schedule their work in the background. The App does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

9. Your Choices

  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, change what you allow at any time in Settings › Ad choices. If you decline, Firebase doesn't collect usage statistics and ads aren't personalized.
  • Ad personalization: on Android you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device). You can also manage Google's ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.
  • Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads stops banner ads and ads between levels. Reward videos are only shown when you choose to watch one.
  • Your nickname and photo: change or remove them any time in Multiplayer › Your profile.
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the App or clearing its storage removes everything saved on your device.

10. Data Retention

Google Analytics for Firebase keeps event data for up to 14 months, and Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. Google AdMob keeps data as described in Google's policies. We don't keep any other copy.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the App and want information removed, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

12. Security

Information sent to Google AdMob, Firebase and Google Play travels over encrypted (TLS) connections. Wi-Fi race messages stay on your local network. Because your progress is stored on your device, we recommend keeping your device secure with a screen lock and up-to-date software.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

14. Contact Us
