Privacy Policy

Arrowmio: Tap Puzzle Game · Android & iOS · Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Arrowmio: Tap Puzzle Game on Android and iOS (the "App"), provided by Horizon Builds ("we," "our," or "us"). The App is free to play, supported by advertising and optional in-app purchases. Platform services differ as described below.

1. Summary

  • No Horizon account is needed. Optional platform features use your Google Play Games or Apple Game Center account.
  • Local saves and platform services: progress and preferences are saved on your device. Google Play Games can also save progress online on Android. Apple Game Center processes achievement progress on iOS.
  • Advertising and diagnostics: Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics process device, interaction and technical information as described below, subject to applicable settings and consent.
  • Platform purchases: Google Play handles Android payments and Apple handles iOS in-app payments. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Local Wi-Fi races: room participants receive your chosen nickname, optional profile photo and race status over the local network.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores game progress, coins, boosters, unfinished puzzles, settings, purchase entitlements and your local multiplayer profile on your device. If you choose a profile photo using the system photo picker, the App stores a small copy for your profile; it does not need access to your entire photo library.

Deleting the App or clearing its storage removes its local data, subject to your device's backup and restore settings. This does not automatically delete platform purchase records, achievements, cloud saves or information already processed by service providers.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob for banner ads, interstitial ads and optional rewarded videos on Android and iOS. Google's advertising SDK may process:

  • Device identifiers, including Android advertising identifiers, the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) when authorized, and app- or developer-scoped identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it
  • Device and app details, ad impressions, clicks and other product interactions
  • Crash reports, performance metrics and other technical diagnostics

This information supports advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, frequency limits and fraud prevention. Ad personalization depends on your region, consent and device settings. Non-personalized ads may still involve data processing. Read how Google uses information from partner apps and Google's iOS advertising SDK disclosure.

4. Usage Statistics and Crash Reports (Firebase)

Google Analytics for Firebase helps us understand game usage. Events may describe screens viewed, level outcomes, booster use, ad interactions, purchases and local multiplayer activity. Analytics may process an app instance identifier, device and app information and approximate location. These identifiers can distinguish an installation even when you have not created a Horizon account. We do not intentionally attach your local multiplayer nickname or profile photo to analytics events.

Firebase Crashlytics helps diagnose failures through crash stack traces, device and operating system details, installation identifiers and diagnostic logs. Reports can include game context such as the current screen, level and recent events. Collection follows the App's applicable consent settings. See Firebase's privacy information.

5. iOS Achievements (Apple Game Center)

On iOS, Arrowmio uses Apple Game Center for achievements. When you use this feature while signed in, achievement identifiers and completion progress are sent to Apple and associated with your Game Center profile. Game Center can make a player identifier, nickname and avatar available to the game for its platform features.

Apple processes Game Center activity under its own privacy policy. Visibility of your achievements and profile depends on your Game Center sharing settings. You can manage those settings or sign out in your device's Game Center settings. This achievement integration does not mean that all iOS puzzle progress is backed up by Game Center. Read Game Center & Privacy.

6. Android Achievements and Cloud Saves (Google Play Games)

On Android, Google Play Games supports sign-in, achievements, game statistics and cloud saves. When signed in, the App sends achievement and event progress and selected save data to Google. Cloud saves include the level reached, coin and booster balances, counted game statistics and certain progress flags, so progress can be restored across installations. They do not include your local multiplayer photo or nickname.

Google processes this information in connection with your Play Games profile. You can manage sign-in and game data through Google Play Games settings. Local deletion alone does not remove the cloud copy, which can restore progress when you sign in again. See Google's Privacy Policy.

7. In-App Purchases (Apple App Store and Google Play)

On iOS, purchases use Apple's in-app purchase system (StoreKit). On Android, purchases use Google Play Billing. The relevant store handles payment and maintains its purchase records; the App does not receive your card number or your Apple or Google account password.

The App processes product identifiers, purchase or transaction identifiers, purchase status and entitlement information to deliver products, prevent duplicate grants and restore eligible purchases. These records may be stored on your device. Purchase-related analytics, such as the product, value and currency, may also be processed by Firebase when collection is enabled. Consumable products, such as spent coins or boosters, may not be restorable in the same way as non-consumable purchases.

Apple and Google retain payment and transaction information under their own policies and applicable legal requirements. For Apple's practices, see App Store & Privacy. Use the relevant store's purchase and refund controls for billing requests.

8. Consent and Advertising Choices

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present consent messages where required, including the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Where available, open Settings › Ad choices to review or change your choices. Consent preferences are stored on your device and used to configure the Google services in the App.

On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency authorization is separate from Google's consent form. Access to IDFA and tracking across other companies' apps or websites require your permission where Apple requires it. You can manage permission in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Denying tracking permission does not itself disable all advertising, analytics or diagnostics.

On Android, advertising identifier and ad privacy controls are available in your device's Google or Privacy settings. You can also manage Google ad preferences at adssettings.google.com. Available controls vary by operating system and region.

Purchasing Remove Ads disables banner and interstitial ads. Optional rewarded videos remain available when you choose to watch them.

9. Local Wi-Fi Races and Permissions

When you host or join a race, devices exchange your chosen nickname, optional profile photo, room information and race progress directly over the local network. This room data is not uploaded to a Horizon multiplayer server. Other participants can see it, so avoid using sensitive information in your profile. Analytics may separately record race events as described above.

On iOS, local network permission allows the App to discover or connect to nearby race rooms. On Android, network, Wi-Fi and multicast capabilities support these connections. Network access also supports ads, diagnostics, platform achievements, cloud saves where supported and purchases. Vibration is used for haptic feedback where available, and the system photo picker lets you choose a profile image.

The App does not request access to your microphone, contacts, camera or precise GPS location for these features. Ad-related identifiers remain subject to the platform controls described above.

10. How Information Is Used and Shared

Information is used to operate the game, deliver purchases, provide achievements and supported cloud saves, show and measure ads, diagnose issues, improve gameplay and respond to support requests. Apple and Google receive data for their services, and local race participants receive the room information described above. We do not sell your local multiplayer profile or provide it to advertising services.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send passwords or payment card details. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect users and the service.

11. Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains until it is deleted, replaced or removed with the App, subject to device backups. Analytics and diagnostic retention depends on the service and our configured retention settings. Platform achievement, cloud save and transaction records are retained under Apple's or Google's policies and applicable legal requirements. Support messages are kept as needed to handle the request and meet legal obligations.

You can change or remove your local multiplayer profile in the App. Manage Game Center and Play Games data using the relevant platform controls. To request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, contact us below. Depending on your region, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent or complain to your data protection authority. We will explain any verification or retention limits that apply. We cannot directly erase every record controlled independently by Apple or Google; their privacy controls and request channels may also be needed.

12. International Processing and Security

Apple and Google may process information in countries other than where you live, subject to their privacy policies and applicable transfer safeguards. We use the platform providers' secure services for purchases and achievements. Local Wi-Fi racing is not end-to-end encrypted; use a trusted network. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

13. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps. Parents can also use Apple's or Google's parental controls to manage platform services and purchases.

14. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. The revised policy will appear here with an updated date.

For privacy questions or data requests, contact Horizon Builds at pramith303@gmail.com or tsabhiram2004@gmail.com. You can also find our contact details on the Contact & App Support page.
