Privacy Policy

Arrowmio: Tap Puzzle Game · Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Arrowmio: Tap Puzzle Game mobile application (the "App") provided by Horizon Builds ("we," "our," or "us"). The App is provided free of charge and is supported by advertising.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect any personal data. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information.
  • No account is needed. You can play without signing up or logging in.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob is operated by Google and may collect certain device information to serve ads, as described below.

2. Information We Collect

Horizon Builds does not collect, store or transmit any personal information about you. We do not run our own servers for the App and we do not receive information about how you play.

Your game progress, such as completed levels and settings, is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to us, and it is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC, to display advertisements. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may automatically collect and process information from your device, which can include:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and language
  • Ad interaction data, such as ads viewed or tapped, and diagnostic information

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud and abuse. Depending on your settings and region, the ads you see may be personalized or non-personalized. Horizon Builds does not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

To learn more about how Google handles this information, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • How Google uses information for advertising

4. Your Choices

  • Ad personalization: On Android you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Google ad settings: You can manage how Google personalizes ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Deleting game data: Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage removes all progress saved on your device.

5. Permissions

The App only uses the permissions needed to load ads: Internet and network state access, and, where applicable, access to the Advertising ID used by Google AdMob. The App does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data, because we do not collect any. The only third party that receives device information through the App is Google AdMob, as described in Section 3.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Horizon Builds does not collect personal information from any user. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Security

Because your game progress is stored only on your device, we recommend keeping your device secure with a screen lock and up-to-date software.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
