Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the following applications (hereby referred to as "the Apps") provided by Horizon Builds:

  • AR Drawing Sketchflow
  • AR Drawing: AnimeRyu

1. Introduction

Horizon Builds ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our AR drawing applications. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that all data is kept secure and local.

2. Data Collection (Offline First)

Our AR drawing applications are built with an Offline-First architecture:

  • No Accounts: You do not need to create an account or sign up to use the Apps.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information, such as your email address, name, or phone number.
  • Local Processing: All drawings, sketches, edge-detection conversions, and user settings are stored and processed strictly on your local device. We do not upload your artwork or photos to our servers.

3. Third-Party Services

The Apps may use trusted third-party services that process data on our behalf for advertising and analytical stability:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and ad interaction data to show relevant ads.
  • Google Play Services: Required for application distribution, licensing, and stability reporting.

4. Permissions Used

To provide core augmented reality and utility features, the Apps request the following device permissions:

  • Camera: Required to capture real-time camera feeds to project and overlay drawing outlines onto physical surfaces. No camera feed is recorded or transmitted.
  • Storage (Photos & Media): Required to let you select reference images from your gallery for outline conversions and to save completed artwork.
  • Internet: Required strictly for AdMob advertisement loading and stability checks.

5. Children's Privacy

The Apps do not collect or request any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, we do not store children's data on our servers.
