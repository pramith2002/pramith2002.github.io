Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Flipbook 2D: Draw Animation app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices. This service is provided by Horizon Builds at no cost and is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application does not require you to provide any personally identifiable information to use its core features. All drawings, frames, and animation projects you create are stored locally on your device's local storage. We do not upload your creations to our servers.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, such as Google Play Services, for the purpose of serving ads or collecting anonymous app crash reports to help us improve stability.

Security

We value your trust in using our Application. Because your drawing data is saved directly to your device's local storage, we recommend maintaining standard device security (like a lock screen) to protect your files.

Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
