Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: January 15, 2026

Expiry Guard ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This document explains how our mobile application handles your information and the terms governing its use.

1. Data Storage (Offline First)

Expiry Guard is designed as an offline-first application. All data you enter—including product names, expiry dates, prices, and notes—is stored locally on your device using a local database (SQLite).

  • We do not transmit your personal data to any external server.
  • We do not have access to the data stored in your vault.
  • If you delete the app, your data is removed from the device (unless you have performed a manual backup).

2. Permissions We Request

To function correctly, the app requires specific permissions:

  • Camera: Used strictly to scan barcodes/QR codes and to take photos of receipts/items. These images are stored locally on your device.
  • Storage (Photos/Media): Used to let you pick existing images or documents (PDFs) to attach to your items.
  • Notifications: Used to schedule local alerts for expiring items. These notifications are generated locally on your device and are not sent via the internet.

3. Third-Party Services

While we do not collect data ourselves, the app utilizes standard libraries to function. These services may collect standard usage data (e.g., crash reports):

  • Google Play Services: Required for core Android functionality.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
